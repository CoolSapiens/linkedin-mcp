TalkToHumans LinkedIn MCP lets Claude, Codex, Cursor, ChatGPT, and other MCP-compatible AI clients search your LinkedIn contacts, network, and conversations.

Use it to find warm leads, hidden opportunities, intro paths, partners, candidates, and follow-up opportunities already sitting inside your LinkedIn network.

Server URL:

```txt
https://api.talktohumans.app/mcp
```

## Why LinkedIn MCP?

Your warmest opportunities are often already connected to you:

- old prospects
- past customers
- founders
- recruiters
- creators
- investors
- partners
- people who can make intros

But LinkedIn makes it hard to search, filter, and actually use your own network.

With TalkToHumans MCP, you can ask your AI assistant questions like:

```txt
Find 20 people in my LinkedIn network who match my ICP.
```

```txt
Find people who could introduce me to founder-led SaaS companies.
```

```txt
Search my LinkedIn DMs for conversations with recruiters I should follow up with.
```

```txt
Create a TalkToHumans contact view for these leads so I can message them.
```

Instead of manually digging through LinkedIn, your AI agent can search structured LinkedIn workspace data and return useful people, context, and next actions.

## What You Can Do

TalkToHumans MCP helps AI clients work with your LinkedIn workspace.

Common use cases:

- Search LinkedIn contacts by name, company, role, tag, location, or relationship state
- Find connected LinkedIn contacts who match a specific ICP
- Search LinkedIn and Sales Navigator conversations
- Identify warm leads already in your network
- Find the best people to ask for introductions
- Discover partnership, recruiting, investor, creator, or referral opportunities
- Create curated contact views inside TalkToHumans
- Update contact notes and tags from your AI workflow

## Example Prompts

### Find Warm Leads

```txt
Use TalkToHumans MCP to analyze my LinkedIn network and find 20 hidden leads that match my ICP.

My ICP is: founder-led B2B SaaS companies with 5-50 employees using LinkedIn for sales.

Return a table with:
- full name
- LinkedIn URL
- company
- why they are a good fit
- warmth score
- suggested outreach angle
```

### Find Intro Paths

```txt
Use TalkToHumans MCP to find people in my LinkedIn network who could introduce me to my ideal customers.

My ICP is: independent recruiters and small recruiting teams using LinkedIn daily.

Return:
- person
- why they are relevant
- who they may know
- suggested intro ask
- confidence level
